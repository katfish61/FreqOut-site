FreqOut

FreqOut is a site-survey tool for production sound on iOS and iPadOS. Before you deploy wireless mics on a film, TV, or theatre job, sweep the UHF band with an RTL-SDR dongle to see what's already occupying the spectrum at the venue — then pick clear frequencies with confidence.

Why FreqOut

Serious RF coordination on iOS has largely meant compromising, or carrying an Android tablet just to run one. FreqOut brings a real spectrum survey workflow — sweep passes, trace stitching, a clear waterfall view — to the iPad already in your kit, using an inexpensive RTL-SDR dongle reached over the network via rtl_tcp. No demodulation, no desktop software: just a sweep of the band and a picture of what's clear.

Built for the UK UHF band

v1 covers 470–1000 MHz, with the UK DTT channel grid (Ch 21–48) and the three main PMSE allocations highlighted: the Ch 38 shared licence (606–614 MHz), 823–832 MHz licensed, and 863–865 MHz licence-exempt.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

Summary

FreqOut does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. No account is required to use the app.

What the app does

FreqOut connects to an rtl_tcp server that you specify — on your own Mac, PC, or Raspberry Pi — and receives a stream of radio frequency samples over your local network. All processing takes place on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No identifiers of any kind

Local network

iOS will ask permission before allowing FreqOut to communicate on your local network. This permission is used solely to reach the rtl_tcp server address you enter in Settings. The app never contacts the internet.

Third-party SDKs

FreqOut contains no third-party code or SDKs. It uses only Apple-provided system frameworks (SwiftUI, Network, Accelerate).

Children

FreqOut is a professional tool not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will be revised. Because no data is collected, changes are unlikely to affect you.
